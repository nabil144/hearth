The sickle

Hesiod, Theogony 154-210 · 5 min

❙❙
1:52 / 5:10Narration. The transcript scrolls with the voice.

Ouranos would not stop. Every child Gaia bore, he pushed back inside her. She groaned under the weight.

So she made a thing. Grey adamant, the hardest stuff there was, shaped into a sickle with jagged teeth. She showed it to her children and asked who would use it. Only the youngest answered.

Kronos waited in ambush. His father came down with the night. The boy reached out with his left hand.
